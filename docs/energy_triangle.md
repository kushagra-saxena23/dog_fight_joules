# The Energy Triangle

The core of "Aces in the Void: Ignition" is the "Energy Triangle," a system that requires pilots to manage their ship's power between three key systems: Speed, Weapons, and Shields.  A pilot's ability to shift power between these systems on the fly is critical to survival and victory.

## The Three Systems

* **Speed:** Governs the ship's top speed and acceleration.  Diverting power to speed allows for rapid repositioning, chasing down enemies, or escaping dangerous situations.
* **Weapons:**  Controls the recharge rate of the ship's primary and secondary weapon systems.  More power to weapons means more consistent damage output.
* **Shields:**  Determines the strength and recharge rate of the ship's shields.  A strong shield can absorb significant damage, but requires a significant power draw.

## The Trade-Offs

The Energy Triangle is a zero-sum game.  Boosting one system will always come at the cost of the others.

* **Boosting Speed:**  Dramatically increases the ship's top speed and acceleration, but slows weapon recharge and weakens shields.
* **Overcharging Weapons:**  Maximizes weapon recharge rates, but reduces the ship's top speed and weakens shields.
* **Reinforcing Shields:**  Significantly strengthens shields and their recharge rate, but makes the ship slower and reduces weapon recharge.
