# Fighter Classes

"Aces in the Void: Ignition" features three distinct fighter classes, each with its own unique strengths, weaknesses, and special abilities.

## Interceptor

* **Role:** A fast and agile fighter designed for dogfighting and taking down enemy fighters.
* **Primary Weapon:** Homing Missiles
* **Secondary Weapon:** "Afterimage" decoy - Creates a holographic duplicate of the ship to distract enemies and break missile locks.

## Bomber

* **Role:** A heavy fighter designed to take down capital ships and other large targets.
* **Primary Weapon:** Proton Torpedoes
* **Secondary Weapon:** "Point-Defense Turret" - Deploys a temporary automated turret that shoots down incoming missiles and small fighters.

## Fighter

* **Role:** A versatile and well-rounded fighter that can adapt to a variety of situations.
* **Primary Weapon:** Rapid-fire Cannons
* **Secondary Weapon:** "Target-Lock Breaker" EMP pulse - Emits a pulse of energy that breaks enemy target locks and temporarily disables nearby enemy systems.
